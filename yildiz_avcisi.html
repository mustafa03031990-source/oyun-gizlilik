Star Catcher — Privacy Policy

Star Catcher does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The game works fully offline, requires no internet permission, contains no advertising and no third-party analytics. Game progress (high score, settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

The game is not directed at children under 13.
